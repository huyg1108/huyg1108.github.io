  Developed an IoT-based smart car control system that translates real-time hand gestures into movement commands. The Python application uses MediaPipe and OpenCV to process webcam input, a TensorFlow Lite keypoint classifier to recognize gestures, and MQTT communication to send Forward, Back, Left, Right, and Stop commands to an ESP32 car controller.
